The Opportunity

FNB Connect already sells the SIM, the device and the data. Protection is the missing piece.

Most telcos have to persuade a customer to trust them with security. FNB Connect starts from a bank's trust, and it already holds the customer's money, phone number and handset. Adding device and identity protection fits that position, and it is a product roughly half of consumers already expect from their provider.

01

1 million SIMs, already billed

02

A device business to attach to

03

eBucks as a payment rail

For a bank-owned MVNO, the handset is the front door

An FNB Connect SIM sits in the same phone as the FNB App, its approvals and its one-time PINs. When that handset is compromised, it is often the first step in account takeover and SIM-swap fraud against FNB's own customers. Protecting the device also protects FNB's fraud line, so this is a defensive investment as well as a revenue one.

Personal Cyber Warranty · by eInsurer

Bitdefender stops the attack. eInsurer covers what still gets through.

No security product stops every attack. eInsurer, DSG's personal cyber division, adds a Personal Cyber Warranty that reimburses customers for losses from a cyber incident. That gives Connect Protect a promise in two parts: Bitdefender works to prevent the attack, and the warranty pays out if something goes wrong anyway. Because FNB Connect's customers bank on the same phone, that second part matters.

What the warranty covers
01

Theft of Funds

Reimburses money lost when a bank account, payment card or mobile wallet is hacked, plus the cost of investigating the incident and taking legal action against whoever was responsible.

02

Identity Theft

Covers lost income, the cost of reapplying for credit and of reissuing ID documents, with up to six months of credit and identity monitoring.

03

Data Restoration & Malware Clean-up

Pays an IT expert to restore data and software, or replace damaged device parts, after an infection.

04

Cyberbullying, Stalking & Reputation

Covers removing harmful content, legal action and reputation support, with an upfront payment for trauma support and school relocation costs if needed.

05

Cyber Extortion

Pays an expert to check whether a ransom demand is real, works to restore systems and data, and covers reasonable costs of resolving it.

06

Network Security Liability

Covers investigation costs, and any legal costs or settlements, when a customer's compromised device harms someone else.
